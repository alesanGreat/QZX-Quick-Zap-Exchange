# QZX name and trademark policy

QZX — Quick Zap Exchange is created and maintained by Alejandro Sánchez.
This policy explains how the project's name, logo, and visual identity may be
used. It does not claim that any mark is registered in every jurisdiction.

## Open-source license and identity

The Apache License 2.0 grants rights to use, modify, and distribute the covered
code. It does not grant general permission to use the QZX name, logo, or visual
identity in a way that suggests an official release, endorsement, partnership,
or sponsorship.

## Uses that do not need prior permission

You may:

- refer to QZX truthfully in articles, reviews, tutorials, research, and talks;
- state that software is compatible with, built from, or inspired by QZX when
  that statement is accurate;
- link to the official website, package, or source repository;
- retain factual attribution and notices required by the software license.

Use only as much of the QZX identity as is necessary to describe the
relationship. Do not imply that a third-party product or organization is
official, certified, sponsored, or endorsed by QZX.

## Forks and modified distributions

A fork or modified distribution must use a clearly distinct name and
presentation. It may state its origin factually, for example, "based on QZX,"
but must not present itself as the official QZX release or use the QZX logo as
its primary identity.

## Sponsors and compatibility claims

Permission to display a sponsor's logo does not make that sponsor a partner,
certifier, or owner of QZX. A future "Compatible with QZX" program, if created,
will use public, reproducible, and non-discriminatory technical criteria.
Payment will never guarantee a favorable compatibility result.

## Logo and other uses

Ask before using the QZX logo in merchandise, advertising, events, domain
names, product names, or any placement that may imply an official relationship.
Contact `qzx@yumbale.com` with the proposed use and context.

code that the license already permits.
