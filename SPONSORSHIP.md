# QZX sponsorship and independence policy

Support may fund maintenance, documentation, security, compatibility testing,
infrastructure, or another clearly described public result.

A sponsor may receive:

- optional, proportionate public recognition;
- a link or authorized logo associated with the supported work;
- an impact report using public or aggregated measurements;
- a presentation of the funded result;
- the right to describe itself accurately as a sponsor of the named work for
  the agreed period.

A sponsor does not receive:

- private user telemetry or personal data;
- control or veto over the general roadmap;
- a guaranteed favorable technical result;
- exclusive access to essential QZX Core features;
- priority over security, privacy, or quality;
- permission to call itself a QZX partner, certifier, or endorsed product
  without a separate written agreement.

QZX will distinguish money, donated infrastructure, promotional credit, and
volunteer time. Support will be accepted only when its source, obligations,
future cost, and intended use are compatible with the project's independence
and the [QZX Core Guarantee](QZX_CORE_GUARANTEE.md).

Questions about sponsorship, infrastructure, cloud or AI credits can be sent to
`qzx@yumbale.com`.
