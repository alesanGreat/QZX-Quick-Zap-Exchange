## Contributors

QZX welcomes code, tests, documentation, translations, compatibility evidence,
design review, and reproducible reports. Public repository history and accepted
contributions remain the source of truth for individual authorship.

Meaningful contributors, reviewers, translators, and sponsors should be
credited in the artifact or report where their work appears. Sponsorship is
reported separately and does not imply authorship, partnership, endorsement, or
control of the project.

If a contribution is missing attribution or is credited incorrectly, contact
`qzx@yumbale.com` with the relevant public reference.
