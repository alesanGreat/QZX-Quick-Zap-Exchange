# QZX community code of conduct

QZX welcomes people who help through code, tests, documentation, translations,
compatibility evidence, design review, issue reports, or respectful technical
discussion.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Make room for different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and
  accessibility needs.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, security-reporting boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted
  contact.
- Credit contributors, reviewers, translators, and prior work accurately.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation, repeated disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in QZX repositories, issue trackers, review threads, project
events, and other spaces where someone is representing QZX. Technical
disagreement is welcome; abusive conduct is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a private conduct report, email `qzx@yumbale.com` with the subject
`[CONDUCT] QZX report`. Include the relevant links, dates, context, and the
safest way to follow up. Do not put sensitive personal information in a public
issue.

Alejandro Sánchez, as lead maintainer, will review reports as privately and
promptly as practical, consider conflicts of interest, and choose a
proportionate response. Possible actions include a private clarification,
warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from
project spaces. Immediate safety risks may be escalated to the relevant
platform or authorities.

No one receives a guaranteed outcome because they are a maintainer,
contributor, sponsor, customer, or critic. Good-faith reports and appeals will
be evaluated on the available evidence.
