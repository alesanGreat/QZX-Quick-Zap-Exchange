# QZX Core Guarantee

**Version 1.0 — effective 24 July 2026**

QZX Core will remain free and open source. Organizations may pay for work,
operation, support, or assurance around QZX, but not to unlock essential CLI
features.

This means:

- the QZX CLI and official public commands remain available under an
  open-source license;
- public schemas, basic documentation, and examples remain available without a
  paid account;
- essential backup, preview, recovery, and safety mechanisms are not premium
  features;
- QZX does not sell individual telemetry or private user data;
- sponsors do not receive veto power over security, privacy, licensing, or the
  general roadmap;
- generally useful improvements funded by a customer or sponsor are published
  in QZX Core when contractual and privacy obligations allow it.

Optional paid services may include hosting, managed compatibility testing,
support or service-level agreements, organization-specific integrations,
training, compliance work, and private reports based on an organization's own
systems. Those services charge for time, operation, responsibility, and
convenience; they do not make the local CLI deliberately incomplete.

This is a public project commitment, not an additional software license. Any
future revision will be versioned, dated, explained publicly, and reviewed
against QZX's open-source mission.
